Delivery and Returns

Delivery

  • Delivery within 5 working days from the order date.
  • Delivery fee is Rs. 350. Delivery is free when the items total (after discount) is Rs. 7,000 or more.
  • Cash on delivery fee is Rs. 150.

Returns: time limits

  • Tell us the reason within 48 hours after you receive the order, and send photos on WhatsApp.
  • Return the item within 5 days after you receive it.
  • The hours and days are counted from the day you receive the order.

If the mistake is ours

  • If you received a wrong or damaged item, send photos and your order number on WhatsApp within 48 hours.
  • We will exchange the item or refund the money, and we pay the delivery charges.

If you want a change

  • If the size is too small, too big or different, choose another size from the stock we have at that time.
  • You pay the full delivery charges, both ways.
  • If the size you want is out of stock, we refund the price of the item. Delivery charges are not refunded.

Condition of returned items

  • Items must be in the original condition: tags attached, not dirty and not damaged.
  • Inner wear and used items cannot be returned.
  • Electrical items can be returned only if they are faulty.

Refunds

  • Refunds are made within 5 working days.
  • The money is sent only to a bank account in the name of the person who placed the order.
